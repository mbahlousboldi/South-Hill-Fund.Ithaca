PORTFOLIO ARCHITECTURE

The three funds

Three student teams. Three investment mandates. One experiential learning program.

Each fund is run by a different student group, with its own mandate, benchmark, and risk profile.

THE EXPERIENCE

Built like an investment firm

Students move from analysis to accountability in a professional environment.

01

Research

Students analyze companies, securities, markets, and macroeconomic conditions using professional tools.

02

Pitch

Investment ideas are presented, challenged, and defended before faculty, alumni, and industry professionals.

03

Manage

Approved ideas become portfolio decisions, creating a direct connection between classroom analysis and investment outcomes.

04

Communicate

Students learn to explain performance, risk, valuation, and strategy with the clarity expected in the profession.

About the program

What the fund is, and where to go for course information.

The South Hill Fund is the umbrella for Ithaca College School of Business's student-managed investment portfolios, based in the school's trading room. Students research holdings, defend theses to faculty and alumni, and manage live capital across equity, fixed income, and growth mandates — building the same skills used on real trading floors, from the NYSE to asset managers across the industry.

Looking for course information?

For details on the Equity Analysis, Fixed Income, and related courses, visit the official Ithaca College School of Business site.
